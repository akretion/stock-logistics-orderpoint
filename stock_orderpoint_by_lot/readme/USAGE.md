On product template, check the "replenish_by_lot".
Create an orderpoint for the product.

Click on the "Order lot" button.

For products replenished by lot, the Forecasted Report includes a lot selector.
Choose **All lots** to compare separate lot curves, **No lot** for quantities
without a lot, or a named lot to filter the graph, totals, and movement details.
Opening the forecast from a reordering rule selects that rule's lot automatically.
